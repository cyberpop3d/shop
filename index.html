ABOUT THE COLLECTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an AMS to print these models?

No. The multipart designs separate colors into printable components, so you can print them without an automatic material changer.

How does collection access work?

Choose a collection and send a direct message to @cyberpop3D on Instagram. We currently arrange payments manually while website checkout is being completed.

Where can I find recommended print settings?

CyberPop Studio's print guide and slicer profiles are available at landing.cyberpopstudio.com.
