LICENSE FAQ

Plain-language guidance. The applicable published legal document controls if there is any conflict.

Can I print CyberPop models for myself?

Yes. Personal/non-commercial hobby printing is the default permission unless a product states otherwise.

Can I share the STL with a friend?

No. Digital redistribution is not included, even when no money changes hands.

Can I upload files to Telegram, Discord, Hot4Share or a shared drive?

No. Uploading or sharing the original or modified digital files through file hosts, groups, torrents, shared drives or private communities is prohibited.

What if I modify the STL?

Resizing, splitting, adding supports, repairing, remeshing, converting or sculpting changes does not create digital redistribution rights.

Can I sell physical prints?

Only where a valid CyberPop Seller License or product-specific commercial physical-print permission applies. Digital files themselves may not be redistributed.

Does a Seller License give me third-party character or trademark rights?

No. CyberPop can grant only rights it owns or is legally entitled to license.

What happens if a rights holder asks CyberPop to remove a design?

CyberPop may place the product under legal review, suspend sales and downloads, remove it, or block the relevant property from future publication after a verified request.

How do I report pirated CyberPop files or unauthorized physical sales?

Use the Rights Center. CyberPop reviews reports before taking platform or provider actions.
